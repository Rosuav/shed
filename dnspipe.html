Traceroute to $$destination$$

What even IS a traceroute?

Traceroute is a network debugging tool that can tell you how information gets from your computer to some other computer on the internet. Usually you will see a series of hops tracing a path from, say, your computer to your wifi to your ISP to the country where the server is to that server's location and finally to the server itself. Sometimes, some of the hops will be masked, and some traces never successfully complete, but this is a valuable tool for exploring the internet.

Run this command to generate the trace. On Windows, use tracert $$destination$$ /h 255 instead.

The header tells us where we're going to be tracing to, including its IP address.

In the early part of the trace, we find out how to get from the source to where the interesting part begins. This section will look very different for you.

How long did it take to get a response? Three probes were sent and the times are reported. An asterisk means that a probe didn't come back.
